About

A mother and daughter who come to you.

Relais Studio is Melina and Fonny, the mother-and-daughter team behind Me + Lia, the Melbourne label making natural-fibre dresses and loungewear for American customers since 2022. Relais is what happened when the places we love to stay started asking for our robes.

  • We visit you. We bring samples to US venues in person, and stay your contact from first brief to reorder.
  • We know the fabric. Years of designing in cotton and linen taught us what washes, wears and sells.
  • We've done it before. Robes for Alila Bali's gift store, and guestwear for Siwa Lombok.

"We started making the things we wanted to find in the places we love to stay."

Meet the makers

Two workshops. Around 24 makers.

We work with two partner workshops in Indonesia, one in Bali and one in Bandung, West Java, each with a team of about twelve. Modern machines for cutting and embroidery, skilled hands for sewing and finishing.

Questions

Before you enquire.

The things venues usually ask first. Anything else, just write to us.

What's the minimum order?

25 pieces per item in our house colours, or 50 per item once your logo, embroidery or custom label goes on. You choose which pieces to carry, so a first order can be as small as one item.

How long does production take?

Around 6 to 8 weeks from approved samples and deposit to dispatch, plus shipping to your venue. Tell us your opening or launch date and we'll plan back from it.

Can we see samples first?

Yes. For most venues we can send a few unbranded samples of the pieces you're considering at no charge. Branded samples, with your logo or embroidery, are charged, and that cost is credited against your first order.

Can you add our name or logo?

Yes. Your name or logo goes on the label, and on the pouch and ribbon for packaged pieces. We can also adjust colour and fit on each order.

Do you ship to the US?

Yes, we ship to venues across the United States. Freight and any import duties are quoted with your order, so you know the delivered cost before you commit.

How is pricing worked out?

It depends on the pieces, fabric, quantities and finishing. Send us your brief and we'll come back with a written quote and sample options.

Will reorders match?

Yes. We keep a record of the fabric, colour and label for every order, so your second run matches the first.
