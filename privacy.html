Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Relais Studio makes guestwear and wellness essentials for venues. This page explains what we collect through relaisstudio.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, venue name and location, the pieces and quantities you're considering, your timing and any message. We don't use cookies for advertising, and the site doesn't track you across other websites.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and quote, and look after any order that follows. We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't add you to a marketing list without asking.

Who handles it

Enquiry forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, and our email is hosted by Spaceship. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services process data only to provide that service.

How long we keep it

For as long as we're talking about or fulfilling an order, and afterwards only as long as we need for our records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Email hello@relaisstudio.com.
